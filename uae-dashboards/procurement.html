How the numbers work

Negotiated savings = first quote minus final PO value. Price vs market compares each PO with the benchmark rate in its month, and copper-linked items (cables, copper pipe) move with LME copper. Budget prices the same quantities at rates fixed when each project started, so copper inflation shows up as overrun.

On-time delivery counts delivered POs that arrived by the need-by date. Maverick spend is spend with no approved requisition before the PO.

UAE industry dashboards

Four interactive dashboards on four UAE industries. Each one opens with the answer a manager would want first, written from the data, and the answer rewrites itself as you change the filters.

  • Where in Dubai does property pay the best rent for the price?Real estate, with a chat you can question in plain English. About 3,100 sales across eight communities: price per sq ft, rental yield, off-plan share and buyer origin.
  • How much is cash on delivery really costing a UAE online store?E-commerce. Revenue, returns, payment methods and delivery speed across all seven emirates, with Ramadan and White Friday peaks.
  • Which funds are beating their benchmark, and which clients are walking away?Asset management. Assets, net flows, performance against benchmark, client mix, liquidity and a call list of clients who are pulling money out.
  • Which delivery lanes break their promise, and why?Logistics. On-time delivery by lane, causes of delay, cost per kg and transit times from Jebel Ali, Dubai South, KEZAD and Sharjah.

About the data

Every figure is synthetic. The data was generated in Python to follow the real shape of each market, such as Ramadan and White Friday peaks, the summer slowdown, rising off-plan sales and slower cross-border lanes. None of it comes from a real company or government source.

The dashboards are plain HTML with custom SVG charts and no external chart library, so they load quickly on a phone.
